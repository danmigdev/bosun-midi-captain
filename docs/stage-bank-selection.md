# Configure Stage bank selection

Tap or click **BANK** to open the fullscreen bank grid. Scroll to see more banks.
The selector beside **X** chooses how a bank selection behaves:

| Setting | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Load rig immediately** (default) | Loads the same rig number in the selected bank when available, otherwise its first available rig. |
| **Preselect bank** | Returns to Stage showing that bank's rigs while the current sound keeps playing. |

The same setting applies to **+ / -**. It is saved in the current browser or app.
Changing the setting alone does not load a rig.

## Preselection

After selecting a bank, Stage highlights the pending selection and shows which
bank and rig are still playing. Tap one of the displayed rigs to load it and
finish the selection. **CANCEL** returns to the playing bank without changing
the sound. The Captain's physical controls continue to operate normally.

A disconnection, profile change or physical rig change clears the pending
selection. Closing and reopening Stage also clears it. With reduced motion,
the highlight stays steady instead of pulsing.

Close the bank grid with **X** or Escape to leave without selecting a bank.
The dropdown also supports arrow keys and Enter; Escape closes the dropdown
before closing the dialog.
